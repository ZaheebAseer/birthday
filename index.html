Happy Birthday,
Madam jii. ♡

Teri aankhon mein jo thehra hai, woh khwaabon ka safar lage,

Teri muskurahat se roshan har udaas sa shehar lage.

Tere chehre pe noor aisa, subah ki pehli kiran jaisa,

Tujhe dekhe toh chaand bhi kuch pal ko be-asar lage.

Teri baaton mein narmi hai, kisi geet ki rawaani si,

Teri khamoshi bhi kabhi dil ko ek pyari kahaani si.

Teri zulfon pe shaam thehre, teri hansi pe phool khilein,

Tere qadam jahaan bhi jaayein, raaston pe rang milein.

Magar husn sirf chehre ka ho, toh baat adhoori hai,

Teri fitrat ki roshni hi teri sabse pyari noori hai.

Tera dil jo khwaab sambhaale, woh sab ek din sach ho jaayein,

Teri aankhon ke saare aansu dua ban kar beh jaayein.

May you be loved without pretending, understood without explaining,

May peace find you in the quiet, and hope keep gently remaining.

Teri har jeet pe khushiyon ke naye phool khilaaun main,

Teri thakan bhari shaamon ko duaon se sajaaun main.

Tu apni nazron mein hamesha apni qeemat pehchaane,

Koi duniya kuch bhi keh de, tu khud ko kam na jaane.

Meri zindagi ke safhon mein tera zikr ek narmi sa hai,

Jaise lambi raat ke baad subah ka pehla ujala sa hai.

Main teri kahaani ka har mod apna keh nahi sakta,

Par tere liye nek dua karna kabhi chhod nahi sakta.

So on your birthday, may every gentle wish come true,

May life return the kindness that the world receives from you.

Aaj ka din tera hai, har khushi tere naam rahe,

Teri hansi salamat rahe, tera dil aabaad rahe.

Aur jab kabhi yeh din guzre, bas itna yaad rakhna,

Kisi ne dil se chaha hai ke tu hamesha khush rahe. ❤️

May this year bring you peace, courage, and happiness you never have to force.

With all my heart,
